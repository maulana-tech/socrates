SIGAP — Supervised Swarm Architecture

A supervised multi-agent system on Amazon Bedrock, targeting a live SAP S/4HANA Cloud sandbox rather than a simulated backend. Ten agents share one blackboard and hand off peer-to-peer; the supervisor admits them and decides when the evidence is enough. No two disruptions activate the same set.

Reading the diagram

Supervised swarmNot a pure swarm — the supervisor holds the goal and the audit trail, which a contract-bound business requires. Not a pipeline either: specialists share context and hand off directly to each other.
Rust arrowsPeer-to-peer handoff, no supervisor round-trip. Inventory Integrity finding a quality hold pushes Impact Analyst to recompute the stockout date on its own.
◆ VetoCompliance eliminates options outright. In the Ningbo event the cheapest candidate died here, not in the cost model.
Dashed bandsThe blackboard and the integration layer. Everything above the lower band is untouched when the sandbox is swapped for a customer tenant.
TKDN
Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri — Indonesia's local-content ratio. OEM supply contracts set a floor, commonly 40%.
LARTAS
Larangan & Pembatasan — the import-licensing regime. A new country of origin adds ~10 working days.
TANJUNG PRIOK
Jakarta's port, Indonesia's main container gateway. Dwell time is variable, not a constant.
OTIF
On-time-in-full — the delivery measure OEM customers score their suppliers on.
JISDOR · BMKG · INSW
Bank Indonesia's reference rate · the meteorological agency's open data · the single-window customs portal.
