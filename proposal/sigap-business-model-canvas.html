Value Propositions

Decide in twenty minutes what currently takes three days — and never propose an option that breaks the contract.

  • Speed is the saving. The cheap mitigation expires while a planner is still assembling the picture. Deciding on day one instead of day three cuts the expedite premium 38% — Rp 70 million per event, ~Rp 980 million a year per plant pair
  • Compliance-safe by construction. Options breaching TKDN (Indonesia's local-content ratio) or LARTAS (its import-licensing regime) are vetoed before they reach a human. A cheaper answer that voids an OEM contract is not an answer
  • Protects the OEM scorecard — delivery rating drives next year's allocation, and it is worth more than the freight bill
  • Runs inside the existing SAP landscape. No replacement, no data migration — an adapter against published OData APIs
  • Bounded autonomy. Acts alone on low-value moves, drafts and escalates on everything else. Deployable in a contract-bound business

Customer Relationships

  • Land: paid 8-week pilot on one material family, with a measured baseline agreed before we start
  • Expand: material category → plant → group
  • Quarterly business review reporting verified savings per event — renewal is argued with the customer's own numbers
  • Assistive, not replacing. The planner approves and stays accountable; this is what gets the system past internal resistance
  • Enterprise CSM above 3 plants; self-serve constraint updates below

Channels

  • SAP Store and AWS Marketplace — procurement is already approved there
  • SI partner channel — primary route to volume
  • Direct enterprise sales for the top 50 accounts
  • Association pilots via GIAMM and industry events
  • Hackathon Demo Day — first proof point and first introductions

Customer Segments

  • Beachhead — Tier-1 automotive component manufacturers on the Karawang–Cikarang–Bekasi corridor running SAP. High import dependency, JIT contracts, TKDN obligations. Roughly 200 firms
  • Second — Tier-2 suppliers into the same OEM chains, reached through Tier-1 reference
  • Third — FMCG, pharma and electronics manufacturers with the same import exposure and the same TKDN rules
  • Later — ASEAN markets with local-content regimes: Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia. The constraint engine is the part that localises

Economic buyer — Supply Chain Director
Daily user — material planner, buyer

Cost Structure — largely fixed; the variable component is small and falling

  • Engineering and domain team — the dominant fixed cost
  • Regulatory research keeping the constraint pack current — the cost that earns the subscription
  • SAP certification and partner fees
  • Bedrock inference — variable, per disruption event handled
  • Cloud infrastructure — Lambda, DynamoDB, Knowledge Bases: modest, event-driven
  • Sales and implementation — mostly carried by the partner channel
Unit economics. One disruption event costs an estimated Rp 100–200 thousand in inference and infrastructure to handle end to end, against Rp 70 million of expedite premium avoided — roughly a 400:1 ratio. Gross margin is set by the subscription, not by usage, which is what makes outcome-based pricing safe to offer. Estimate — to be measured during enablement.

Revenue Streams — priced against avoided cost, not seats

  • Platform subscription — per plant per month, tiered by active material count. Rp 45–120 million / plant / month
  • Implementation and SAP integration — one-time, Rp 150–400 million, usually delivered by the SI partner on a revenue share
  • Constraint pack subscription — regulatory updates, Rp 8 million / month. Low value, high retention: it is the reason nobody churns
  • Gain-share option — 15% of independently verified expedite savings, for buyers who want the risk on us. Attractive because the baseline is measurable per event

A single plant pair avoiding Rp 980 million a year comfortably carries a Rp 60 million/month subscription. The pitch is not a licence fee — it is a share of a loss that is already being paid, quietly, fourteen times a year.
